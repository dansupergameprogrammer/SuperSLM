no default. The generator substitutes the stem, refuses a missing file, and hashes exactly that file into
`discrimination_record_sha256`. Only `superslm_precache_reference_gen` changes. The loader, `e4_real.cpp` and
`superslm_precache_e1` are untouched: e1 rebuilt at `47bdcc9e` is `2531182b…5d26`, byte-identical to `e8c029ed`'s.
