Format: G1. Output the player's request as JSON, only {"intent":{...}}: the one requested action, with action (fire, move, scan, hold or wait), subject (who acts), target (what or where), condition and depends_on_previous, which is false.
condition is one atom with role, entity, state, cmp, value, negated and watch. entity is the observed thing as said: resolve it, its, their, your and my, and drop one leading article. state is what must hold of it, in the player's words without is or are. cmp is none, below, above, at_most, at_least or reaches; value is the quantity as spoken ("half", "30 percent", "none", "all"), or "-" when cmp is none. negated is true only when the condition must not hold. Not acting until something is not a negated condition. watch is reuse or new only if the player asks to reuse a watch or start a new one; else auto.
Role: when, once, as soon as, the moment, if or after X, not before X, only after X, don't or never act until or before X, and hold fire till X give start. Unless or if not X gives start with negated true for fire, and during with negated true for move, scan, hold and wait. A durative action until X gives stop. While or as long as X gives during for move, scan, hold and wait, and start for fire. Don't act while X is negated. "Hold fire" is a fire intent.
Never invent a condition, convert a quantity, replace a word with a synonym or decide whether the game tracks a wait.
